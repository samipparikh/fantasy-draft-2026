        What the model is doing

        Method. Each matchup gets an expected points-allowed line from three inputs: an offense index for the opponent, a defense index for the defense, and home field. Those three coefficients are not guesses — they are fit by least squares against the closing betting lines for the — team-games that have them, so the whole scale is the market's. Expected tier points are then integrated over a normal centred on that line, because a defense projected to allow twenty points does not allow exactly twenty and the tier table is a steep staircase. A sack-and-takeaway component, league-average at — points and scaled by the same two indices, is added on top.

        • The defense index is the league's own board, extended. The draft export ranks the defenses it contains from DEF1 to DEF31, and the eleven undrafted teams are exactly the eleven missing rank slots. Ordering those eleven by last season's points allowed and reading their projection off the rank curve keeps all thirty-two on one scale. A prior built from last season alone rated Cleveland an above-average defense; the league's own board has it seventeenth. Note where that leaves the top free agents: Cleveland, New Orleans and Carolina land at DEF17 through DEF19 by construction, immediately below the last drafted defense, so their edge is the least certain number on this page. It is also 45 points wide over the Giants, which is more than the imputation could plausibly be wrong by.
        • Betting lines only exist through week eight. Weeks nine to eighteen use the same fitted coefficients with no market to check them against, so they are a ranking of matchups rather than a forecast of points. Hover any cell that has a line to see it.
        • This is a matchup model, not a simulation. No injuries, no weather, no pass-rush rate, no snap-level anything. It ranks weeks within a defense and defenses within a week, which is all a start-or-sit decision needs.
        • Scoring is the standard points-allowed table plus 1 per sack, 2 per takeaway, 6 per return score. If this league scores defenses differently the ordering barely moves, but the point totals will.
